Engine analysis uses Stockfish 19, launched as a separate UCI process.
The Windows binary, GPL license text, authors list, and corresponding
source are under stockfish19/.

Project: https://stockfishchess.org/
Source: https://github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish
License: GNU GPL v3 (see stockfish19/Copying.txt)

project, available under the Open Database License (ODbL):
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/
